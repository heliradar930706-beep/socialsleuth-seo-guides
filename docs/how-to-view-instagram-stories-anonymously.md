## What Does Anonymous Viewing Mean?

In this context, "anonymous" means the viewer does not require you to sign in with your personal Instagram account to look up available public Stories. This differs from opening a Story directly from your signed-in Instagram account.

It does **not** promise absolute anonymity on the internet. A website may still use routine technical information for service operation, abuse prevention, and security. Review the service's privacy notice for details.

## How Long Do Instagram Stories Last?

A typical Instagram Story is available for around **24 hours** after it is posted, unless the creator removes it sooner. When it expires, a live public viewer cannot simply reconstruct it.

This is why checking a Story once and keeping an archive are different tasks. Archiving can preserve material captured while it was still accessible; it cannot guarantee recovery of a Story that disappeared before it was saved.
